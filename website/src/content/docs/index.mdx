## What this is

A deterministic four-stage forensic pipeline over a WordPress newsroom corpus.
It produces statistical and documentary signals — change-points in stylometric
features, embedding drift, convergence to an "AI baseline" — that reviewers can
inspect, reproduce, and audit. The outputs are **signals, not legal findings or
definitive attribution of authorship or tool use**.

## Responsible use

This project quantifies stylistic and structural shifts in published text. It
does **not** identify the author of a piece, prove the use of a specific tool,
or render a legal verdict. Reviewers should pair every reported signal with
domain context, editorial records, and corroborating evidence before drawing
conclusions.
